# WARDLINE INDIA — Guided Research Presentation Mode Guide (v1.3.0)

## Overview
Presentation Mode provides a full-screen, live-data narrative experience for guided demonstrations of the historical analysis. Unlike static slide decks, every chart inside Presentation Mode consumes the active dataset (`official` MoHFW HMIS or `imported_unverified` CSV) and remains interactive during live Q&A.

## Routes
- **5-Minute CA1 Demo Route (6 Chapters)**: Chapters 1 (Introduction), 2 (Research Problem & Scope Honesty), 5 (Live Historical Trends), 6 (Live Seasonal Clock), 9 (Leakage-Free AI Forecasting), and 10 (Verified Findings).
- **Full Technical Walkthrough (12 Chapters)**: Adds Dataset Provenance (Ch 3), Cleaning & V09 Coverage Observatory (Ch 4), Regional Disparities & V07 Dispersion (Ch 7), 3D Spatial Lab Bridge (Ch 8), Limitations & Responsible AI (Ch 11), and Conclusion & Future Work (Ch 12).

## Keyboard Shortcuts & Controls
- `ArrowRight` / `Space`: Advance to next chapter
- `ArrowLeft`: Return to previous chapter
- `N`: Toggle Presenter Speaker Notes drawer
- `Esc`: Exit Fullscreen (or exit Presentation Mode back to the dashboard)
- `Motion: Reduced` (inherited from global shell / `prefers-reduced-motion`): Disables progress-bar and chart transitions immediately.
