# Research limitations

- The verified source reports **service utilization**. General all-cause admissions, facility-tier splits and patient demographics are unavailable. Institutional deliveries cover an obstetric service and OPD counts describe outpatient attendance.
- Administrative reporting and completeness vary by month, geography and indicator. Ratios and fixed-cohort summaries use explicit denominators. The counts do not estimate total clinical demand, disease prevalence, or comprehensive nationwide hospital volume.
- The archive covers April 2012 through March 2020. FY 2019–20 evaluation is historical and retrospective. The data cannot validate forecasts for current service operations.
- The 34 harmonized areas are continuity units for analysis. Regional comparisons use reporting-unit series. The public edition excludes boundary geometry and population-normalized comparisons.
- The project reconciles to a third-party HMIS derivative. Original government workbook authenticity was outside the verification scope. Faculty review of academic scope is separate from software testing.
